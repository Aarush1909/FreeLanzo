A DIFFERENT WAY

FreeLanzo is a student-built freelance portfolio platform with a simple idea: make it easier for people with talent to meet people with ideas.

We're creating a welcoming space where work speaks for itself, different disciplines meet, and collaborations can begin. The journey starts with a place to showcase your work.
