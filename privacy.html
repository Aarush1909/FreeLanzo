FREELANZO / PRIVACY

Privacy Notice.

Phase 3.1 student-project draft · Updated 15 November 2026

This is a student-project draft. Review it against your actual data collection and legal obligations before public launch.

1. What we collect

When you register, Firebase Authentication processes your email address, sign-in provider and authentication credentials. FreeLanzo stores your account ID, email, display name, account type, signup date, and terms acceptance metadata in Cloud Firestore.

2. How we use information

We use these details to authenticate you, remember your session if you choose, identify your Freelancer or Client account type, and prepare your account for future platform features.

3. Profile photos

Members can choose a profile photo. When Cloudinary is configured, selected photos are optimized to a 400 × 400 square and uploaded to Cloudinary for hosting. The browser checks a maximum 500 KB file size. Image delivery URLs are saved in Firestore. Removing a photo from FreeLanzo removes its reference in the app, but does not automatically delete the stored asset from Cloudinary; the project operators must manage Cloudinary assets separately.

4. Public freelancer profiles

Only freelancers who choose to publish appear in Explore. Published details include the chosen avatar, display name, headline, bio, location, skills, category, and portfolio project information. Email and private account data are not copied into these public records. We attempt to hide contact details inadvertently entered into public text, but members should avoid putting contact details in publicly shared fields. Signed-in users may view these read-only profiles; clients do not have public profiles.

5. Google sign-in

Google sign-in uses Firebase Authentication and Google’s identity service. FreeLanzo does not ask for access to your Gmail inbox and does not receive your Google password.

6. Data access and security

Private account and editable portfolio records are owner-only. A separate, allowlisted public profile and project collection is readable only to signed-in members while the freelancer has opted in. We do not put passwords in Firestore.

7. Retention and contact

A proper public privacy policy must state retention, account deletion options and a working contact channel. These processes are not implemented in this student-project phase.
