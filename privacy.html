FREELANZO / PRIVACY

Privacy Notice.

Phase 4.3 student-project draft · Updated 18 November 2026

This is a student-project draft. Review it against your actual data collection and legal obligations before public launch.

1. What we collect

When you register, Firebase Authentication processes your email address, sign-in provider and authentication credentials. FreeLanzo stores your account ID, email, display name, account type, signup date, and terms acceptance metadata in Cloud Firestore.

2. How we use information

We use these details to authenticate you, remember your session if you choose, identify your Freelancer or Client account type, and prepare your account for future platform features.

3. Profile photos

Members can select and crop a profile photo. FreeLanzo checks that the final cropped image is between 10–200 KB in the browser before uploading the processed image to Cloudinary. The image URL is stored in Firestore. Removing or replacing a photo does not automatically delete the Cloudinary asset; the project team must manage hosted images separately.

4. Public freelancer profiles

Only freelancers who choose to publish appear in Explore. Published details include the chosen avatar, display name, headline, bio, location, skills, category, and portfolio project information. Email and private account data are not copied into these public records. We attempt to hide contact details inadvertently entered into public text, but members should avoid putting contact details in publicly shared fields. Signed-in users may view these read-only profiles; clients do not have public profiles.

5. Google sign-in

Google sign-in uses Firebase Authentication and Google’s identity service. FreeLanzo does not ask for access to your Gmail inbox and does not receive your Google password.

6. Data access and security

Private account and editable portfolio records are owner-only. A separate, allowlisted public profile and project collection is readable only to signed-in members while the freelancer has opted in. We do not put passwords in Firestore.

7. Private messages and work offers

Text messages, inbox unread counters and work offers are stored in Cloud Firestore. Chat media attachments are not supported. Work offers contain a client name/company, payment amount, agreed working schedule, work description, deadline and payment terms. Signature images (up to 30 KB) are stored within private offer records and are visible to both conversation participants. Clients may cancel pending offers; freelancers may accept or decline them. Offer terms remain recorded and cannot be edited after sending. Conversations are protected by Firebase security rules, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. Recipients can retain copies of anything shared. FreeLanzo does not verify signers’ identities, guarantee legal enforceability, or process payments.

8. Blocking

Participants can block one another to prevent new messages and work offers in either direction. Previous chat history remains available to both participants. This version does not include user reporting, admin review, or automated moderation.

9. Email verification

New email/password registrants must verify ownership of their address before using the dashboard or messaging features. Existing accounts and Google sign-in accounts continue under their previous verification requirements.

10. Retention and contact

You can request account deletion from your dashboard Settings. Because deletion is performed by client-side steps, interruptions can leave data behind, including Cloudinary image assets. Shared chat messages, work offers and signature images remain available to the other participant after deletion. FreeLanzo closes those conversations and replaces the deleted account’s inbox display name; this does not remove personal information that was included in messages or offers. A public launch needs reliable server-side deletion, clear retention periods and a working support contact.
