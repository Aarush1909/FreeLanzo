Phase 2 student-project draft · Updated 14 November 2026

This is a student-project draft, not a professionally reviewed legal policy. Review and adapt it before any public launch.

1. About FreeLanzo

FreeLanzo is a student-built freelance portfolio and discovery project. At this stage, it supports account creation and sign-in. Portfolio, search, and messaging features will be added in later phases.

2. Accounts

Provide accurate information, keep your password private, and use your own account. At registration you choose Freelancer or Client; this selection is attached to your account. Do not impersonate others or misuse the platform.

3. Acceptable use

Do not upload unlawful, abusive, misleading or infringing content, attempt unauthorized access, or interfere with other users' accounts or the website.

4. Content and intellectual property

You remain responsible for materials you choose to share. Do not post work you do not own or lack permission to use. Any future public portfolio features will require appropriate content rules.

5. Service limitations

FreeLanzo is under development and may change, pause, or contain bugs. There are no payment or booking functions in Phase 2.

6. Contact and changes

Before launch, the project team should add a real support contact, legal jurisdiction, and version-change process to these terms.
