Phase 4 student-project draft · Updated 17 November 2026

This is a student-project draft, not a professionally reviewed legal policy. Review and adapt it before any public launch.

1. About FreeLanzo

FreeLanzo is a student-built freelance portfolio and discovery project. It supports accounts, profiles, portfolio projects, freelancer search, real-time text messaging and client-created work offers. Chat file attachments are not supported.

2. Accounts

Provide accurate information, keep your password private, and use your own account. At registration you choose Freelancer or Client; this selection is attached to your account. Do not impersonate others or misuse the platform.

3. Acceptable use

Do not upload unlawful, abusive, misleading or infringing content, attempt unauthorized access, or interfere with other users' accounts or the website.

4. Content and intellectual property

You remain responsible for materials you choose to share. Do not post work you do not own or lack permission to use. Any future public portfolio features will require appropriate content rules.

5. Service limitations

FreeLanzo is a student project under development and may change, pause or contain bugs. In-platform payments and verified electronic signature services are not available. Chat and work offers are student-project features, not payment or identity-verification services.

6. Work offers and signatures

Work offers can include a client name or company, payment amount, working schedule and uploaded signature image. Freelancers can accept or decline them. These actions do not verify either party’s identity, arrange payment, or guarantee legal enforceability. If you require a binding commercial agreement, obtain independent legal review and use an appropriate verified signing process. Keep sensitive personal or financial documents out of chat.

7. Contact and changes

Before launch, the project team should add a real support contact, legal jurisdiction, and version-change process to these terms.
